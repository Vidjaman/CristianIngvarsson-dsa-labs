Why can keys 1 and 6 both exist when the table has five buckets?
Because the modulo ensures they all fit in one of the buckets
1%5=1
6%5=1

What happens to lookup cost if almost every key ends up in the same bucket?
it becomes the same as a linear search O(n)

Why does expected O(1) lookup not mean "exactly one CPU operation"?
because sometimes it has to do more than one CPU operation per cycle for instance in a hashmap it has to calculate the bucket index.

Why must the original key still be stored even after we have calculated its bucket?
To tell the diffrence between collisions
